How we work

How we turn vision into patient growth

A disciplined 4-stage operational framework engineered specifically for private medical and aesthetic clinics.

01

Discovery & Unit Economics Audit

We begin with a clinical diagnosis of your practice: average patient lifetime value (LTV), treatment room capacity, and procedure margin distribution (Botox, Morpheus8, surgical vs non-surgical). We locate exactly where your front desk is leaking high-value inquiries.

02

Bespoke Conversion Funnels & Authority

Generic websites leak 95% of traffic. We engineer fast, conversion-focused landing pages that showcase the physician’s credentials, clinical before/after results, and educate patients on procedure outcomes — driving them to pay consultation deposits online.

03

AI Speed-to-Lead & EHR Integration

82% of patients book with the first practice that responds. We deploy our AI Concierge to respond in under 45 seconds via SMS & WhatsApp, pre-qualify patient intent, and book directly into Jane, Aesthetic Record, Boulevard, or Nextech.

04

Launch, Scale & 90-Day VIP Recall

We launch high-intent search campaigns and local Google 3-Pack domination. Simultaneously, we install automated 90-day neurotoxin recall loops that re-book existing patients automatically, generating predictable recurring revenue.
