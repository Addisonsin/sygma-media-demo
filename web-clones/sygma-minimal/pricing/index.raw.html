All FAQs

How much does SEO cost for an aesthetic clinic in the United States?

Our clinic SEO starts at $999 a month, and for a little more most clinics are better served by a Search Growth program, which combines SEO, AI search and website management: Presence at $999 a month for a single clinic, Authority at $1,686 for a clinic in a competitive city, and Flagship at $2,856 for groups, clinics in the most competitive markets and surgical practices.

What is AI search optimization and what does it cost?

AI search optimization, also called AEO or GEO, is the work of making your clinic the source that ChatGPT, Google AI Overviews, Perplexity and Gemini read, trust and cite when patients ask about treatments. On its own it starts at $715 a month, as an add-on to an SEO retainer from $455 a month, and a one-time AI search audit starts at $1,105. It is built into every Search Growth program.

Why do you recommend SEO and AI search together?

AI engines lean heavily on pages that already rank and on businesses they can verify across the web. Run separately, the two disciplines duplicate research and pull in different directions. Run together, every page is written to rank and to be quoted, and one strategist is accountable for both.

Is there a minimum contract?

No. Every retainer is rolling monthly with 30 days' notice. If you prefer to pay upfront, six months saves 10% and twelve months saves 15%. You can pay securely by card through Stripe straight from this page, and cancel before your next billing date at any time.

Are there setup fees?

There is no setup fee on retainers. Your first month covers onboarding: the technical audit, the keyword and prompt map and your 12 month roadmap. Project work such as websites and branding is quoted as a fixed price before we start.

What does website care include?

Hosting, SSL, backups, security and uptime monitoring, updates, and a set number of hours each month for edits such as price lists, team changes and offers. Care is $162.50 a month, Manage $383.50 and Priority $643.50 and one of them is included in every Search Growth program. Unused hours do not roll over, and larger changes are quoted before we start.

Can you track bookings from my booking system?

Yes. Every program connects your booking system to your website tracking, so inquiries, calls and bookings are measured from the start. Authority traces each booking back to the page and search that won it, and Flagship reports bookings for every location by page, channel and campaign. Where a booking platform cannot be connected directly, we track the booking step on your website instead.

Do the programs include backlinks and conversion work?

Yes. Every program earns quality backlinks and brand mentions each month: one on Presence, three on Authority and six on Flagship, plus a quarterly digital PR campaign on Flagship. Every link is earned from relevant, reputable sites, never from link networks. On conversion, Presence gets a UX review at onboarding with quick fixes in your edit hours, Authority gets a quarterly UX review followed by design updates, and Flagship gets monthly reviews, design updates and A/B testing on your money pages.

What is Fill Your Calendar?

Fill Your Calendar is our add-on for clinics with uneven weeks. We connect to your booking system and read how full each day is, never who is booked. Once a week we look at the seven days ahead, and if one day is clearly quieter than your normal it becomes that week's last-minute availability day: a short notice goes on your website and an email goes to your patients. If tomorrow is still exceptionally quiet, a stronger 24 hour note follows. If every day is healthy, nothing is sent. It is availability, never a discount, so it fills the day without training patients to wait for offers. $383.50 a month on its own, included in Flagship, with an $1,105 email set-up only if your clinic has no list yet.

Is advertising spend included in the price?

No. Ad spend is paid directly to Google or Meta, and platform subscriptions such as your CRM are paid to the provider. Our management fee is fixed and is never a percentage of your spend.

Do you guarantee rankings or AI citations?

No reputable agency can, because nobody controls Google or the AI engines. We commit to the work, the evidence behind it and a transparent monthly record of what was done and what moved.

Can I change plan later?

Yes. You can move up at any time and move down with 30 days' notice. A natural path is Presence first, then Authority once the foundations are in place.

How do I pay?

Choose a program or website plan and press start: you pay securely by card through Stripe, and we start your onboarding within one working day. If you would rather talk first, book a call and we will send a written quote and a payment link.
