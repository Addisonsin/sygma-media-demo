Pricing questions

Who writes and publishes the blog posts?

We research, write, optimise, and publish in your CMS (or provide ready-to-publish drafts if you prefer approval first). You don’t need to supply briefs unless you want to.

What’s the difference between Launch, Growth, and Scale?

The strategy stack is the same. Scale changes how much we produce each month: GBP posts, new pages, on-page optimisations, and support intensity.

Do we need a new website?

No. Plans assume an existing site we can optimise. If you need a rebuild, start with the one-time Foundation build, then move to monthly SEO.

How does cancellation work?

Plans are month-to-month after onboarding. To cancel, send written notice (email is fine) at least 30 days before you want the partnership to end. You're billed for any work in that notice period; we don't charge separate "break-up" fees. We'd rather earn next month's retainer than argue about fine print.

Do I sign a long contract?

No annual or multi-year agreement required. Some clinics choose Growth or Scale for years — that's because results compound, not because a contract forces it.

Want a line-item proposal?

Tell us your city, treatments, and consult goals. We’ll map deliverables to Launch, Growth, or Scale and send a scoped PDF.
