About us

We build the digital engine that fills your consult calendar

Sygma Media is a U.S.-focused growth partner for med spas, dermatology, and plastic surgery practices. We don't sell generic "marketing packages" — we ship the search, Maps, website, and AI visibility patients use before they ever call your front desk.

If you're tired of agencies that report impressions while consults stay flat, you're exactly who we built this for.

Why clinic owners choose Sygma

You're not buying slides. You're buying a pipeline: rankings that match how patients search, a site that earns trust in seconds, and Maps presence that wins "near me" — with reporting that ties work to consults, not vanity metrics.

Only aesthetics

We don't split focus across restaurants, law firms, and e‑commerce. Every playbook is tuned to treatment research, compliance-aware copy, and how patients actually choose a provider.

Senior delivery

Three U.S.-based founders cover strategy, SEO/web, and social execution — so your account never disappears into a junior queue.

No handcuffs

Month-to-month after onboarding. Cancel with 30 days' notice. We'd rather earn the next retainer with results than trap you in a multi-year contract.

What we ship every month

  • Local & organic SEO — treatment pages, blogs, and on-page fixes that match how patients search in your city.
  • Google Business Profile — posts, categories, photos, and Q&A so Maps reflects a premium practice.
  • Website conversion — speed, trust blocks, and booking paths that turn traffic into form fills and calls.
  • AI & structured data — entity copy and schema so ChatGPT, Perplexity, and Google AI can cite you accurately.
  • Transparent reporting — what went live, what moved in Search Console and Maps, and what we're doing next.
  • Portfolio-proof mindset — we document before/after visibility and consult trends like the cases in our portfolio.

Three founders, one accountable team

Sygma Media was built by three founders based in the United States — one owns client strategy and delivery, one leads search, web, and technical SEO, and one runs social and content execution. You get direct senior attention without a single-person bottleneck or a rotating cast of account managers.

We're happy to introduce the team on a call before you commit. Names and photos can live on a dedicated team page when you're ready to publish them.

You're a great fit if…

  • You run (or are launching) a med spa, dermatology, or plastic surgery practice in the U.S.
  • You want consult growth from organic search and Maps — not only paid ads.
  • You value clear monthly deliverables over endless strategy decks.
  • You're willing to approve content and give us CMS/GBP access so we can actually ship.
