What is an aesthetics marketing agency?+

An aesthetics marketing agency specializes exclusively in digital marketing for aesthetic clinics and plastic surgery practices: covering SEO, website design, Google Ads, social media, and content marketing. Unlike generalist agencies, a specialist understands FTC compliance requirements, treatment-specific search intent, and the patient journey that drives bookings in the US market.

How much does aesthetics marketing cost for a US clinic?+

Most US aesthetic clinics invest $999–$2,499 per month with a specialist aesthetics marketing agency. Packages typically include SEO, content creation, and paid media management. Sygma Media offers SEO retainers from $999/month and full clinic launch infrastructure from $4,500.

Do I need a specialist aesthetics marketing agency, or will a generalist work?+

A specialist will consistently outperform a generalist. FTC and state board advertising rules restrict how aesthetic treatments can be advertised, particularly injectables. A generalist without aesthetics experience will produce non-compliant content or water down your messaging until it fails to convert. Specialist knowledge of treatment keywords, patient intent, and competitor dynamics means measurably faster results.

Can you help me launch a new aesthetic clinic from scratch?+

Yes. It is one of our core services. Our pre-launch digital packages build your complete digital ecosystem before opening day: website, SEO foundation, Google Business Profile, social media, and content strategy. Clinics with digital infrastructure in place before launch rank faster and acquire their first patients sooner.

Can you help my clinic rank in AI search and Google Maps?+

Yes. It is what we specialize in. We work to get aesthetic clinics across the U.S. recommended by ChatGPT, Google AI Overviews, AI Mode, Gemini and Perplexity, and into the Google Maps results for their treatments. The work covers SEO, structured data, a complete Google Business Profile, consistent listings, genuine patient reviews and the clinic's wider authority online. Miami Luxe MedSpa is cited by ChatGPT and Perplexity for Aerolase laser in New York, and Manhattan Dermatology Partner for hair loss treatment in New York Metro.

Do you have case studies from aesthetic clinics?+

Yes. We publish our work as case studies rather than quotes, and every clinic we name is a real client. Yes. We work with aesthetic clinics and plastic surgery practices right across the U.S., from Miami to Seattle, and every service is available nationwide. For clinics that want to win their own town or city, we build local SEO and Google Maps visibility around each location.
